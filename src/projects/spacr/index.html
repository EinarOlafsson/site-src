spaCR

Spatial phenotype analysis of CRISPR-Cas9 screens.

spaCR segments cells, nuclei and pathogens in microscopy images, measures every object, and links the measurements to the guide RNAs sequenced from the same wells. It starts from plate images and FASTQ reads and ends with per-cell measurements, trained classifiers and a ranked list of genes.

What spaCR does

Seven modules, run in order. Each one reads the previous module's output and writes its own into the same project folder. Segmentation, measurement, annotation and classification also run without sequencing data.

  1. ImportConverts microscope files into one folder layout per plate.Produces: Image folders and a project
  2. MaskSegments cells, nuclei, parasites and organelles with Cellpose models.Produces: Label masks for every field
  3. MeasureMeasures shape, intensity, texture and position of every object.Produces: measurements.db and single-object crops
  4. AnnotateLabels object crops in a keyboard-driven grid.Produces: Labels in the project database
  5. ClassifyTrains image models (ResNet, MaxViT) or measurement models (XGBoost, random forest).Produces: Checkpoints with held-out metrics, per-object scores
  6. Map barcodesDecodes row, column and guide barcodes from FASTQ reads.Produces: Guide counts per well
  7. RegressionEstimates guide and gene effects from scores and guide counts.Produces: Volcano plot and ranked hit list
  8. And moreDose-response, control charts, plaque and recruitment assays, image UMAP, batch correction, power and plate design, and a QC dashboard. See Assays and QC and Phenotypes.

Inputs

  • TIFF, OME-TIFF, ND2, CZI and LIF images, 2-D, volumetric and time series
  • Multi-well plates from any microscope that writes one of these formats
  • FASTQ reads with row, column and guide barcodes
  • Live folder watch for ND2, CZI, LIF and OME-TIFF files as they are written (alpha, off by default)
  • OME-Zarr and OMERO through optional extras

Organisms

  • Toxoplasma gondii in host cells: guide, recruitment, plaque and replication assays
  • Any cell type for segmentation, measurement and classification
  • Pages for other pathogens are in development and hidden by default

Outputs

  • An SQLite database per project: measurements, labels, predictions
  • Masks, crops and images as files on disk
  • Effect estimates, p and q values, diagnostics and run records
  • Figures as PNG, PDF, SVG or TIFF; one zip per figure with its data, statistics and a script that redraws it
  • AnnData export, and a vvvv export (alpha)

Models

{{STAT_MODELS}} published Cellpose-SAM and YOLO models with model cards: parasite vacuoles, plaques, plaque-assay wells, and cross-channel models that predict cells, nuclei or parasites from a single stain. Weights download from Hugging Face and are checked against a published checksum. See Models.

Pipeline

One whole field followed through spaCR: HeLa cells infected with Toxoplasma, plate 2, well P08, field 16 of the MTOC screen, imaged with Hoechst and CellMask. Steps 1 to 4 use this field; step 5 is the hit list of the GFP-TSG101 example screen, because the MTOC screen's hits are not public.

  1. 1Image

    13 host cells and 54 vacuoles in this window of a 2000 × 2000 px field; Hoechst blue, CellMask grey.

  2. 2Mask

    Cell, nucleus and vacuole outlines: 37 cells, 44 nuclei and 130 vacuoles in the field.

  3. 3Measure

    Cells coloured by vacuole coverage (Measure): 9 of 13 above 0, up to 0.161.

  4. 4Classify

    XGBoost infection score from host features only; 8 of 13 cells above 0.5, field not used for training.

  5. 5Hit

    Four-plate GFP-TSG101 screen: 414 genes, 4 called at q < 0.05; GRA14 is the hit.

Data: Hoechst and CellMask images with published cell, nucleus and vacuole masks from cross-channel-cell-from-hoechst, cross-channel-nuclei-from-cellmask and cross-channel-toxoplasma-from-cellmask (CC BY 4.0). Measure and the XGBoost classifier ran in spaCR on CPU over 52 fields of the same plate. Hit list: OLS regression of spacr-example-screen.

  • MaskSegments cells, nuclei, pathogens and organelles with Cellpose models.
  • MeasureShape, intensity, texture and position of every object, and a crop of each, in SQLite.
  • AnnotateLabels object crops in a grid with keyboard shortcuts.
  • ClassifyTrains image or measurement classifiers and stores held-out performance with each checkpoint.
  • Map barcodesAssigns sequencing reads to wells and guide RNAs.
  • RegressionEstimates guide, gene and condition effects with intervals and a ranked hit list.

Permutation tests

spaCR's guide-permutation analysis (Regression, nonparametric inference) on the screen's MaxViT per-cell scores: each gene's and each guide's partial correlation with the well-mean score, given plate, row and column, against 2,000,000 within-plate Freedman–Lane permutations (seed 0). Layout as in the preprint's Figure S7 (panels R and S). With the example's shipped settings, control wells and guides stay in, so the numbers differ from the preprint, which excluded them and filtered wells. Data: spacr-example-screen, settings from spacr-example-hit.

Phenotypes

Every measured cell can be placed on a two-dimensional map with its image crop, so that groups of similar cells can be found and checked by eye.

Classification

Classifiers that tell the screen's control wells apart, trained and explained with spaCR on CPU: an XGBoost model on per-cell measurements and a small image model (MobileNetV3-small, 8 epochs) on single-cell crops. Column 1 wells carry guides against SAG1 (negative control) and column 2 wells against GRA14 (positive control). Wells are held out whole, so no test cell shares a well with a training cell. Data: spacr-example-screen and spacr-example-hit, plate 1, CC BY 4.0.
