Biography
Einar B. Olafsson is a Research Investigator in the Department of Microbiology & Immunology at the University of Michigan. He studies how Toxoplasma gondii interacts with the cells it infects, combining CRISPR screening, high-content microscopy and AI-based single-cell image analysis to link genetic perturbations to spatial cellular phenotypes. He created and maintains spaCR. He studied biology and immunology at Uppsala University and received his PhD in Molecular Biosciences from Stockholm University, with a thesis on signalling in Toxoplasma dissemination by infected immune cells.
