Cellect phone mount

A 3D-printed mount that holds any phone over any microscope eyepiece, so Cellect can image through the eyepiece with the phone camera.

Phones 57–99 mm wide Eyepieces 18–47 mm OD X/Y ±18 mm
Cellect on GitHub Print it

How it works

  • Collar (blue): a ring with three thumbscrews that grips the eyepiece. How high you clamp it sets the camera-to-eyepiece gap, so there is no separate focus mechanism.
  • Plate (dark): the phone lies on it screen-up with its camera over the window. A corner stop means the phone goes in the same place every time.
  • Jaw (orange): slides in a slot and pushes the phone into the corner.
  • Cross-slots: the plate slides in X and Y on the collar's flange, with two bolts. The plate edges point at X and Y scales engraved on the flange, so each phone and microscope setting can be written down and repeated.

Phones 57–99 mm wide including the case, with the camera 6–42 mm from the phone's top and side edges.

Set up at the microscope

  1. Focus the sample by eye. Fold down or remove the rubber eyecup.
  2. Slide the collar over the eyepiece and snug the thumbscrews lightly.
  3. Put the phone screen-up into the corner, slide the jaw in, tighten.
  4. Open the camera on the 1× lens. Loosen A and B, slide the plate until the bright circle is centred, tighten.
  5. Slide the collar up or down until the circle is largest and its edge sharpest, then tighten the thumbscrews.
  6. Write down the X and Y scale readings for this phone and eyepiece. Next time, set the same readings and only fine-tune.

Test plan for v0.1

  1. Print one knob and the jaw first to check bolt and nut fits.
  2. Full print, fit on the lab microscope, record the settings, image a haemocytometer.
  3. Remove and re-mount the phone 5 times with the same readings; the circle centre should move by less than about 2 % of the frame width.
  4. Try a second phone and a second microscope.
