TimePose

Segmentation and tracking of cells in time-lapse microscopy, divisions included. TimePose extends Cellpose with flows along the time axis and new output heads.

  • Backbone Cellpose-SAM
  • Variants TimePose-LM, TimePose-FL
  • Manuscript in preparation

Time as an axis

Cellpose describes each cell as a flow field: every pixel points towards the centre of its cell, and following the flows groups pixels into masks. TimePose adds time as a further axis. For every pixel of a frame it also predicts where the same cell was in the previous frame and where it will be in the next.

New heads on the same network score which objects in neighbouring frames are the same cell, and which cell has divided into two daughters. Masks, tracks and lineages come out of one model rather than a segmentation step followed by a separate tracker.

Two variants

TimePose-LM: label-free light microscopy

For unstained cells imaged in DIC, phase contrast or brightfield. It is trained on label-free data from several species, microscopes and magnifications, and the tests include microscopes and magnifications left out of training.

TimePose-FL: fluorescence

Fluorescent signal fades while a movie is recorded, so late frames are dimmer and noisier than early ones. TimePose-FL detects photobleaching and can correct for it, and it is trained to carry evidence from the early, bright frames to the late, faint ones. Left on auto, it is meant to leave movies without bleaching unchanged.

Aims

These are the goals the manuscript has to meet, not results. Each is tested only on held-out data (datasets that TimePose never saw in training, and unseen movies from datasets it did) against a named control.

  • TrackingMore accurate tracks than Trackastra, the strongest tracker in our pilot comparisons.
  • DivisionsMore accurate division detection than Trackastra.
  • SegmentationBetter masks than Cellpose-SAM in time-lapse, by using evidence from neighbouring frames.
  • PhotobleachingBetter masks than Cellpose-SAM on bleached fluorescence movies, also when Cellpose-SAM is given bleach-corrected frames.

Trackastra is compared both as released and fine-tuned on the same training data. Ultrack, btrack, laptrack and frame-to-frame matching are also compared.
