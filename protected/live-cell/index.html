live_cell & cellposeTIME

Cellpose-SAM models for transmitted-light microscopy, and a temporal extension of Cellpose that learns where each cell goes next.

live_cell_v1: segmentation for phase, brightfield and DIC

A cpsam_v2 fine-tune on transmitted-light microscopy (phase contrast, brightfield, DIC and quantitative phase) from 14 public datasets, built for spaCR, where it is available as the zoo model live_cell_v1. Published at epoch 37.

It does not beat stock cpsam_v2 overall, and it missed the promotion bar fixed before it was scored. It is better on the datasets stock Cellpose-SAM never trained on, and worse on LIVECell-style Incucyte phase contrast, which stock was trained on and which dominates the pooled test set.

cellposeTIME: temporal flows

Cellpose represents each cell as a flow field in which every pixel points to the cell's centre. cellposeTIME extends that representation in time: for a window of three frames it predicts, for every pixel of the centre frame, where the same cell was in the previous frame and where it will be in the next one, plus a division channel that links a mother to her two daughters.

Each frame passes through the same pretrained Cellpose-SAM encoder; the three feature maps are fused by a 1×1 convolution initialised to pass the centre frame through unchanged, and the new temporal and division output channels start at zero. Links between frames are decided by the predicted displacement, not by mask overlap, and lineages come out of the same decoding step as the tracks.

One flag, off by default

cellposeTIME is a superset of Cellpose gated by a single --time flag. Without it, nothing temporal is built: three output channels, Cellpose's own loss, and a checkpoint that loads in stock Cellpose.

# plain Cellpose training, Cellpose checkpoint
cellposeTIME --manifest datasets.json --out-dir runs/plain

# temporal model: windows of 3 frames, offsets -1 and +1
cellposeTIME --time --manifest datasets.json \
  --window 3 --offsets -1,1 --dt-choices 1,2,4 \
  --out-dir runs/temporal
