live_cell and cellposeTIME

Cellpose-SAM models for transmitted-light microscopy, and an extension of Cellpose that predicts where each cell was in the previous frame and will be in the next.

live_cell_v1: segmentation for phase contrast, brightfield and DIC

cpsam_v2 fine-tuned on transmitted-light images (phase contrast, brightfield, DIC and quantitative phase) from 14 public datasets. In spaCR it is the model-zoo entry live_cell_v1. The published weights are from epoch 37.

Over the whole test set it scores below stock cpsam_v2 and did not reach the promotion threshold set before scoring. It scores higher on the datasets that stock Cellpose-SAM was not trained on, and lower on LIVECell-style Incucyte phase contrast, which stock was trained on and which makes up most of the pooled test set.

cellposeTIME: flows in time

Cellpose represents each cell as a flow field in which every pixel points to the cell's centre. cellposeTIME extends that representation in time: for a window of three frames it predicts, for every pixel of the centre frame, where the same cell was in the previous frame and where it will be in the next one, plus a division channel that links a mother cell to its two daughter cells.

Each frame passes through the same pretrained Cellpose-SAM encoder; the three feature maps are fused by a 1×1 convolution initialised to pass the centre frame through unchanged, and the new temporal and division output channels start at zero. Links between frames are decided by the predicted displacement, not by mask overlap, and lineages come out of the same decoding step as the tracks.

One flag, off by default

cellposeTIME is a superset of Cellpose gated by a single --time flag. Without it, nothing temporal is built: three output channels, Cellpose's own loss, and a checkpoint that loads in stock Cellpose.

# plain Cellpose training, Cellpose checkpoint
cellposeTIME --manifest datasets.json --out-dir runs/plain

# temporal model: windows of 3 frames, offsets -1 and +1
cellposeTIME --time --manifest datasets.json \
  --window 3 --offsets -1,1 --dt-choices 1,2,4 \
  --out-dir runs/temporal
